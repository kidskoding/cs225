Detailed Description

A closed hashing table with double hashing: h(k, i) = (h1(k) + i * h2(k)) mod m, where h1(k) = k mod m and h2(k) = 5 - (k mod 5). Its size grows to the next prime at least double the old size, so every step size is relatively prime to it
